What's Your Self-Sabotage Pattern?

A 5-minute quiz for people who know what to do—but still find themselves getting in their own way.

You've read the books, taken courses, or done some work on yourself. You can explain your patterns. Sometimes you even catch yourself in the middle of one.

And yet… here you are, doing that familiar thing again.

Let's get curious about what happens between knowing what you want and actually following through.

You'll discover which of four patterns feels most familiar, try one small step, and learn how coaching could help you move forward.

Before you begin

Think about one area where you want something to change: relationships, work, personal goals, or speaking up for yourself.

Keep that area in mind as you answer. Choose one answer per question, based on what you've actually done over the past three months.

Pick the closest fit. No answer needs to describe you perfectly, and there are no “good” answers to collect here.

This is a self-reflection quiz, not a diagnosis or a validated psychological assessment. The names describe patterns—not who you are.
